# Sales-Data-Analysis-Dashboard-in-Excel
Sales Data Analysis and Interactive Dashboard Using Microsoft Excel

1. Project Understanding
I started by understanding the sales dataset and identifying what kind of information it contained. The dataset included details such as Order ID, Order Date, Region, Salesperson, Product Category, Product Name, Units Sold, Total Sales, and Payment Method. The main objective was to analyze the sales performance, understand customer order patterns, compare regional performance, and identify which products and salespersons contributed the most to overall sales.
2. Data Extraction
I imported the raw sales dataset into Microsoft Excel and organized it into a structured format. I converted the data into an Excel Table so that it would be easier to manage, filter, and analyze. I also checked the available columns and made sure the dataset contained the required information for further analysis.
3. Data Cleaning
After importing the data, I checked it for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent entries, and formatting issues. I verified the Order ID, Order Date, Units Sold, and Total Sales columns to ensure the values were accurate. I also checked the spelling and consistency of categories such as Region, Product Category, and Payment Method. This helped prepare the dataset for reliable analysis.
4. Data Processing
Once the cleaning process was completed, I worked on preparing the data for analysis. I extracted the month and year from the Order Date column using Excel formulas. I also organized the records based on regions, product categories, and salespersons. This made it easier to group the data and compare sales performance across different categories and time periods.
5. Data Analysis
I performed sales analysis to understand the overall performance of the business. I calculated total sales, average sales, and units sold. I also compared sales across different regions, analyzed product category performance, and checked individual salesperson contributions. This helped me identify high-performing and low-performing areas and understand the overall sales pattern.
6. Pivot Table Creation
I created multiple Pivot Tables to summarize the dataset and analyze different business metrics. These Pivot Tables helped me compare regional sales, product category revenue, salesperson performance, units sold, and order distribution. I also used them to organize the information into meaningful summaries, making it easier to identify important patterns without manually calculating everything.
7. Data Visualization
After completing the analysis, I created different charts to present the results visually. I used bar charts, column charts, pie charts, and line charts to compare sales across regions, understand product category contributions, analyze salesperson performance, and represent order trends. These visualizations made the results easier to understand and helped highlight important information.
8. Interactive Dashboard Development
In the final stage, I developed an interactive Excel dashboard by bringing together the important charts and sales summaries. The dashboard provides an overview of overall sales performance, regional contributions, product category performance, and salesperson analysis. I organized the charts in a clear format so that users can easily understand the results and compare different business metrics in one place.
Key Business Insights


I. After completing the analysis, I identified several useful insights from the sales dataset.
1. Overall Sales Performance:
The business generated total sales of approximately 1.33 crore from 501 orders, with a total of 5,492 units sold. This provides an overview of the overall sales performance during the available period.

2. Product Category Performance:
Electronics generated the highest revenue, followed by Appliances and Clothing. Stationery recorded the lowest revenue among the product categories. This helps identify which categories contribute more to the business.

3. Regional Sales Performance:
East and South were the top-performing regions in terms of total revenue. North recorded the lowest sales. This comparison helps the business understand regional performance and identify areas that may need improvement.

4. Salesperson Performance:
Eva generated the highest total revenue among the salespersons, while Farah recorded the highest average units sold per transaction. This helps compare individual contributions and understand salesperson performance.

5. Order Distribution:
South recorded the highest number of orders, followed by East, West, and North. This shows how order volume is distributed across different regions.

6. Business Improvement Opportunities:
The business can focus on improving sales in lower-performing regions and identifying ways to increase revenue from categories with lower contributions. It can also study the sales strategies used by high-performing salespersons and apply similar approaches where needed.
